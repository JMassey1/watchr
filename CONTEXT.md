# Watch3r

Watch3r is a collaborative tracker for movies and television shows people intend to watch or have watched together.

## Language

**Watchlist**:
A collaborative collection of titles, owned by a user and shared with its members.
_Avoid_: List, collection

**Title**:
A movie or television show tracked by Watch3r, sourced from TMDB.
_Avoid_: Media, content, item

**Watchlist Item**:
A title's membership in a watchlist, including its watched state.
_Avoid_: Entry, queue item

**Queue**:
The Watchlist Items in a Watchlist that have not yet been watched.
_Avoid_: Backlog, to-watch list

**Watchlist Member**:
A user who belongs to a watchlist with an owner, admin, or member role.
_Avoid_: Collaborator, participant

**Watchlist Activity**:
A change to a Watchlist's items, membership, or metadata made by any Watchlist Member.
_Avoid_: Engagement, usage
